# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Four audiences, confirmed by the owner's representative (Tiago), all arriving mostly on phones:

- **Expatriates and NGO staff living in Dili**, looking for a weekend lunch, dinner or a short escape on the coast.
- **International travellers** planning a trip to Timor-Leste and looking for somewhere to stay outside Dili.
- **Timorese locals**: families and groups coming for meals and celebrations.
- **Event organisers** who want to use the space for birthdays, weddings or gatherings.

Their job on the site: understand what Lauhata is and where it is, see the menu or the suites, and send a request (table, suite, order or event) by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

The website of Lauhata Beach Escape, a beachfront restaurant and bar with two suites in Lauhata, Liquiçá municipality, Timor-Leste, on the coast west of Dili. It exists to turn interest into WhatsApp requests that the team confirms by hand. Success is a visitor who arrives knowing nothing and leaves having sent a clear, complete request.

## Positioning

Four things together, confirmed as the difference:

- **A table by the beach**: restaurant and bar with the sea in front, from breakfast to dinner, and the sun setting over the sea.
- **Small and personal**: a local business run by Carlos Soares and the team; the public reviews single out the welcome.
- **A base for Liquiçá**: close to Dili, a starting point for Maubara Fort, the Aipelo prison ruins and the coast.
- **Eat and stay in one place**: the restaurant and the two suites, all requested through WhatsApp.

## Operating Context

- Every request (table, suite, restaurant order, event, feedback) leaves as a pre-filled WhatsApp message to +670 7740 1111; staff confirm availability manually. Nothing is booked, ordered or confirmed by the website itself.
- Payment is made in person at Lauhata. The website takes no payments and never asks for card details.
- Restaurant orders are for eating in at the restaurant only: no delivery, no takeaway.
- Restaurant hours from the printed menu: breakfast 08:00–10:00, lunch and dinner 11:00–21:00 (days not stated).
- Bilingual: English is the default language (site root); Portuguese lives under `/pt/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generated by `node build.mjs` (no framework, no dependencies), published from `website/dist/` on GitHub Pages; routes in `src/lib.mjs`, pages in `src/pages.mjs`, copy in `src/i18n/`, data in `src/data/`.
- Suites, provisionally named after their beds: **Suite King** (one king-size bed) and **Suite King & Single** (one king-size bed and one single bed). Owner-confirmed amenities: the beds and a television. Wi-Fi, breakfast included, private bathroom and air conditioning are **not confirmed** and must not be published until Carlos confirms them per suite (`pendingAmenities` in `src/data/rooms.mjs`).
- Room prices: not set; the site says "price on request".
- Undecided / pending from the owner: response time to requests, opening days, email and social profiles, the commercial names of the suites, the drinks list and prices, Google Analytics ID, privacy-policy review. Tracked in `docs/CHECKLISTS.md`.
- Analytics (GA4) is prepared but off until an ID is set; it loads only after consent and never counts a click as a confirmed booking.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Lauhata Beach Escape**; the logo supplied by the client (`documentos/marca/`).
- Voice: **warm and factual**. Plain, honest language, no marketing exaggeration, only facts the owner has confirmed.
- Never invent facts, prices, amenities, reviews, ratings, testimonials, statistics or people. Illustrative (non-Lauhata) photos are always labelled as such. Review text is never copied: summaries in our own words, linked to the platform.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real drone footage and stills of the property and the coast (`originais/videos/`, `src/assets/img/fotos/`), and the sunset photograph used in the hero.
- Verified review scores, read on 2026-11-05: Tripadvisor 4.4/5 from 25 reviews; Booking.com 8.2/10 from 4 reviews (`src/data/site.mjs`, summaries in `src/data/reviews-external.mjs`).
- The printed menu (dishes and prices in `src/data/menu.mjs`).
- Address and map pin from the Liquiçá Municipal Authority.
- **Absent, must not be fabricated**: photographs of the real suites, the dishes, the drinks and the team; authorised testimonials; documented events; the story of how Lauhata began.

## Product Principles

1. **A request, never a promise.** Every flow says plainly that the team confirms; nothing on the site may read as a confirmed booking or order.
2. **Only what Carlos confirmed.** Unconfirmed facts stay hidden or marked pending, never filled in.
3. **The place leads.** Real photographs and the setting carry the site; illustrative material is labelled and replaced as soon as real material exists.
4. **One conversation channel.** WhatsApp is the single path for every request, and it should always be one tap away.
5. **English first, Portuguese complete.** Both languages are full versions of the same site, never mixed on one page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first audience on variable connections in Timor-Leste: pages must work on small phones, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep WCAG AA contrast, and stay usable without the background video or heavy assets.
